## How We Assess

### The First 30 Minutes

We walk in, tell the client exactly what we're doing, and run a
network scan. Aggressive timing. No apology.

That scan tells us three things simultaneously:

**1. The technical picture**
What's on the network, what's exposed, what's running that
shouldn't be. Ports, services, OS versions, legacy protocols.
Numbers and facts.

**2. Their detection capability**
Did anything fire? Did an alert appear on a terminal? Did the
firewall log it? Did the EDR catch it?

If nothing caught a loud, aggressive scan — that's not a
vulnerability, that's a story. "An attacker had 5 undetected
minutes on your network." That's the finding.

**3. The security culture**
Who noticed? Who reported it? Who clicked through the warning
and kept working?

The person who calls IT immediately is your security champion.
Find them. Build on them.
The person who ignores it tells you about training gaps.
The machine that never alerted tells you about coverage gaps.

One scan. Three data points. One conversation.

## How We Report

We don't hand clients a spreadsheet of CVEs and walk away.

We tell a story:
- Here's what we found
- Here's what it means for your business
- Here's what an attacker could do with it
- Here's the first step to fix it

**First step only.** Not a 40-page remediation roadmap.
Not a $50K project proposal. One step. Do it together.
Build trust. Then the next step.

---

## Rules We Never Break

**1. We don't blame the current IT.**  
They were working with what they had. We're here to help,
not to make someone feel small. If there's no IT, we say
"here's what we can build together." If there is IT, we
say "here's how we can work together."

**2. We don't sell first.**  
Results earn the relationship. The first engagement is
about showing value, not closing a deal. If we do good
work, the conversation about ongoing services happens
naturally.

**3. We don't hide what we're doing.**  
Every scan, every test, every tool — the client knows.
Transparency is the product. We're not penetration testers
operating in the shadows. We're their partner with better
visibility than they have.

**4. We meet clients where they are.**  
A 5-person office with a consumer router and no IT budget
is not a failed enterprise. It's a starting point. We find
the one thing that matters most to them and we fix that
first. Then the next thing.

**5. Small steps, real results.**  
No big bang projects. No rip-and-replace proposals on day
one. Momentum builds trust. Trust builds the relationship.
The relationship builds the business.
